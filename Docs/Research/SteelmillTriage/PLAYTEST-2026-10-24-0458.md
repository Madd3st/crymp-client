  1342.70) is a node with no edges at all (reached only physically).

  natively it is `goal_unprojected` anyway: no route can ever reach it (Lab: 116 timeouts, 0 pickups).
* Native `no_route` (1032) by component: start in a sink 35 %, goal in a sink or isolated node 35 %,
  goal in an island nothing enters 27 %. Top pairs: sink→main1 249, main1→sink 227, main1→no-entry 225,
  main1→isolated 96. Reproduce: `steelmill_triage.py graph <nav.json> --mem <mem> --replay <bots.log>`
  (fields `scc`, `native_routes`).

Edge acceptance by height difference (all neighbour pairs, both directions): steps 0.3–0.75 m are
kept 87–89 %, flat pairs 82.5 % (axis 87.7 %, diagonal 75.7 %). So the knee ray is **not** the
general culprit; flat pairs without edges are mostly real walls/fences. Which border of the
spawn-195 island is a real obstacle and which is an artefact cannot be decided offline.

Mechanism candidates (src, `BotNav.cpp` @ `b8ddcd22`, not verified in game):
* `Walkable` uses one ray per height (0.5/1.2/1.75) centre-to-centre with
  `ent_static|ent_terrain` and `rwi_colltype_any`: any post or rail on the 3 m line kills the edge, and
  `colltype_any` also hits geometry the player collides with differently.
* Rays are directional (start inside geometry, single-sided meshes), so a→b and b→a can disagree;
  that is how a flat area gets 37 entry edges and no exits.
* One-way edges: 1510; 1042 are legitimate (reverse would be a climb > 1.9 m), 103 are flat. Making
  the flat ones two-way (offline what-if) changes nothing for spawn 195, SMG 199 or pit C: the
  problem is missing edges, not one-way ones.
