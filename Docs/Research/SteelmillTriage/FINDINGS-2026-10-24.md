Definitions (`Tools/SteelmillTriage/wblab/analysis.py`): *pinned* = a whole continuous live

## F1. Spawn point (1247.50, 1319.75, 136.45) pins bots for their whole life — CONFIRMED in logs

17 of 22 bot lives longer than 10 s that started at this spawn never moved, across all
seven logs including the current confirmed build `8f0abd5f`. Lives last until someone kills
the bot: 241, 304, 336 telemetry samples (8–11 minutes) in the owner's 11-Elite match.

Joined `MOTION INTENT` (same telemetry block, see parser) for every pinned life:
`requestedBase > 0` in 100% of samples, `blocked=0` in 100%, measured speed 0.00.
So BotBrain wants to move, `BotMovement::Move` accepts the step (`BotLocomotion.cpp:123`,
`blocked = intent.move && !accepted`), yet the body does not move. The bot still aims,
fires and regenerates; it acts like a turret.

Consequence in code: every stuck reaction is gated on `blocked` (`BotBrain.cpp:416`
patrol, chase-abandon path) or on `wb_progress_experiment` (OFF). With `blocked=0` and zero
displacement nothing ever fires. Short 120 s QA runs mostly miss it (0.0–2.1%); long play
sessions do not (4.1–7.2%).

Not known: whether a human spawning there is also stuck (geometry/physics interpenetration)
or only bots (movement request path). Check with a client at that spawn.

## F2. Pockets with no exit: 23–31 minute traps after travel jumps — CONFIRMED in logs, cause is a hypothesis

| Where | Log | Duration | blocked while requesting |

Bot #1 trajectory: running at z=138.68, at t=1010 z jumps to 141.92 (an airborne sample),
then lands at 139.82 and never leaves; 1091 NAV fallback queries from that spot to local
detour goals 6–10 m around it, all failing. Travel Strength jumps are random
(`BotBrain.cpp:82-83`: every 5–10 s, 70%, gated only by energy/distance/grounded).
Here `blocked=1` and `ReportStuck` fires every 1.5 s, but the only reactions are a memory
penalty and another detour leg. There is no terminal recovery (escape jump back, give up
the area, or a native self-kill after N minutes).

Hypothesis to verify visually: these are surfaces reachable by a Strength jump but
without walkable exits under `BotMovement` safety rules, and with no BotNav node.

## F3. Route failures are mostly vertical — CONFIRMED distribution, cause unproven

`FindPath == false` 15–18 times per live bot-minute in every run. 42–62% of failed
queries have |goal.z − start.z| > 2.5 m (the `Nearest` floor limit, `BotNav.cpp:198`).
16–29% are shorter than 7.5 m horizontally, which can only be projection failures
(`Nearest` searches ±2 columns, `BotNav.cpp:189`). The trace does not say which of
start-unprojected / goal-unprojected / A* budget (6000, `BotNav.cpp:225`) / disconnected
caused each failure. The export contract adds exactly that.

Hottest failing goals (Play11Elite): (1203, 1329, 137) 339 queries, (1287, 1353, 137) 240,
(1224, 1332, 149) 240 — consistent with weapon pickups the owner of `weapon-goals`
already flagged ("weapon 199/161/273 unreachable" at t=10).

## F4. Map memory: half of it is invisible to roaming, and stuck never decays — CONFIRMED in code

From the owner's current `Multiplayer_IA_Steelmill.mem` (7633 nodes, fingerprint matches
the logged grid 96×63, cell 3, min 1121.25/1245.0):

- `PickHotspot` iterates `i += 2` (`BotNav.cpp:297`). Odd node indices are never
  candidates: **51.4%** of fight-weight and 45.7% of safe-weight is ignored. Because node
  order is column-scan order with several floors per column, this also biases against
  upper floors in multi-floor columns.
- Stuck is capped at 20 (`BotNav.cpp:274`), route penalty saturates at stuck ≥ 6
  (`BotNav.cpp:238`, ×4.6 cost). 14 nodes are at full penalty, 5 at the cap; there is no
  decay. Total stuck grew 98 → 231 during the owner's 48-minute match.
- The penalty applies to the node where the bot stood (`Nearest(feet)`), i.e. to every
  route through that spot, not to the edge that failed.
- `ReportStuck` is not logged, so growth cannot be traced to places without the export.

## F5. Dataset is not bandit-ready — CONFIRMED

`TACTIC SAMPLE action=` is the FSM state name in 95–99.7% of rows (`BotBrain.cpp:216-219`;
only `escape`/`withdraw` are real choices). No alternatives, no propensities, rows are also
emitted while DEAD. `BOT SUIT`, `JUMP`, `BOT ACTION`, `ACTION REFUSED`, weapon bursts carry
no actor id (82k lines in the owner's match). `MOTION INTENT` / `ABILITY PHYSICS` can be
joined by adjacency to the telemetry line (same call, same timestamp) — Triage does that.

## What this changes in priorities

F1 and F2 cost more live bot time in real play than any tuning difference measured so far.
They are cheap to detect in-game from data the bot already has (own displacement while
requesting movement). See `PROJECT_COORDINATION.md` for the proposed split.
