## Caveats

- 250 ms sampling can merge rapid bunny-hops into one episode (landing missed):
  episodes with `dist > ~15 m` or `dz > ~5 m` are merged, not single jumps.
- `strength` = peak >= 2.0 m **or** energy drop >= 50 (native cost 70); a merged episode
  can mask the 70-cost with in-flight regen, so a few `strength` rows may be merged normals.
- `drop` = no native jump flag and dz < -1.0 m.
