## Implemented and reviewed

Expanded the existing `BotProfile`, keeping skill and style separate. Beginner has
slower reaction, stable per-life distance errors, slower strafing, no automatic Speed,
and no tactical low-health retreat. Normal/Veteran/Elite increasingly adapt distance
to their own weapon, disengage sooner from blocked hidden pursuit, and withdraw at
own HP thresholds 22/35/45. Elite also withdraws below 60 HP against at least two
visible enemies. Withdrawal uses native Armor/backward movement for 2.5 s with a
5 s cooldown. Speed requires suitable distance, own energy and no recent damage;
existing native Strength actions remain. Assault/Marksman retain different range,
fire discipline and movement styles at the same competence. There is no new cover
planner, verified flank routing or advanced suit combo planner.

`BotCombat::FindEnemy` now scores visible opponents by distance, fit to own weapon,
clear muzzle path/own ammunition, visible facing direction, own HP, and the shooter
of actual recent HP damage. No opponent HP/ammo or hidden live position enters the
score. Candidate LOS queries no longer overwrite selected-target memory. Selection
uses minimum hold times 3/2/1.6/1.3 s and utility margins .45/.35/.28/.25. A held target
is rescanned at 0.6 s; initial target search retains the prior decision cadence.
Hidden positions expire after 3 s. More than 0.5 s visual loss requires fresh reaction;
brief occlusion does not reset the target-hold clock. Target change cannot shorten
an existing burst cooldown. Reaction floors .75/.35/.25/.22 s are further quantized
by the 10 Hz decision loop; native spread and nonzero aim error remain.

Perception remains the inherited common 80 m, omnidirectional LOS system: no new
FOV/hearing model. Health, energy, physics, native damage, standard players, channels,
ShotValidator, GameRules and serialization were not changed in this increment.
Damage notification uses actual HP loss, so Armor-only hits do not yet count as threats.

## Progress experiment: rejected broad policy, final default OFF

Measured requested movement using actual sampled positions: 6 s windows detect
low displacement and looping despite `blocked=0`. Death/revive resets tracking;
respawn displacement never counts as an escape. Stationary/loop flags can also
describe legitimate combat and are not confirmed path failures.

One Universal policy run had 18.02% blocked samples. Concrete visible-target chase
stalls included actor 131017 at `(1317.37,1329.50,144.36)` and actor 131022 at
`(1333.97,1337.13,138.53)`; actor 131027 also stalled while searching. Existing hidden
chase timeout did not cover visible unreachable targets. Exact physics/reload causes
are not yet isolated. Coordinates alone are not a deterministic reproduction.

`BotProgress` measures 1.5 m actual 3D displacement, accepting detours and vertical
movement. The broad `46c95eba` version watched search, weapon and combat tasks:
32 escape events produced 21 same-living-actor horizontal recoveries >2 m within
8 s, 9 nonrecoveries, 2 censored events. Nevertheless Universal kills fell 17 в†’ 11
over two runs. Its two 15-bot runs yielded 84 kills. **Broad policy rejected.**

Final `5e8daa65`: `wb_progress_experiment=0` by default; search/weapon interventions
removed. Optional combat-only watcher protects reload, recent bursts and new targets,
then uses existing nav-memory/local detour plus temporary target avoidance. It never
teleports or rejects all missing-A* routes. Final default 15-bot smoke: 43 kills,
1.45% blocked, .198 ms median update sum, observed flag 0, zero escapes. Two final
Universal 6-bot smokes with explicit flag 1: 8+8 kills, .92/1.84% blocked, **zero
escape triggers**. Gate operation is checked; narrowed recovery efficacy is unproven.
Keep it OFF until a reproducible stall test and balanced same-binary 0/1 comparison.
