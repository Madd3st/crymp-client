Playable source `5e8daa65`; dedicated IA Steelmill, 11 Elite, automatic style by slot,
progress experiment OFF. Real client joined and owner completed the playtest.
Owner cannot yet judge whether Elite is more dangerous than Normal. Earlier match

## Owner observations (not all independently verified)

1. Sometimes killed almost instantly, within roughly a second.
2. Bots appear not to aim for headshots.
3. Bots appear constantly aiming and underuse suit capabilities.
4. Back-and-forth motion/hesitation; little control of sniper positions.
5. Pistol firing seems roughly once per second rather than rapid follow-up shots.
6. Automatic weapons feel excessively lethal at a certain close range.

## Source evidence and hypotheses

- BotCombat::Observe/AimPoint normally chooses the body, with head fallback when
  body LOS is obstructed. This is not proof of zero native headshot hits. Do not
  fix the issue by granting guaranteed headshots.
- BotCombat::Attack uses the same profile burst/gap mechanism irrespective of
  weapon fire mode. SOCOM needs a native semi-auto-specific cadence evaluation,
  not a blanket faster rate or an artificial damage change.
- BotBrain aim error is min(profile cap, distance * error/m), hence shrinks close
  up. Tracking/shot alignment and native damage events should be correlated with
  video before assigning cause to the reported close-range time to kill. Armor,
  health, weapon and concurrent shooters matter; no damage multiplier was changed.
- BotMovement::Aim requests aim/fire/look targets; this does not by itself prove
  native ADS/zoom is enabled. Verify stance/aim/sprint acceptance separately from
  suit intent, actual mode/energy and observed animation.
- No strategic sniper-position occupancy/defence planner exists. Existing Marksman
  range preference is not control of a sniper nest. Movement hesitation/stalls
  remain unresolved; broad progress recovery remains rejected/OFF.
